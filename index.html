📘 Nouns

A noun is a word that names a person, place, thing, animal, or idea.

Examples: teacher, school, book, dog, happiness

🏃 Verbs

A verb is a word that shows an action or state of being.

Examples: run, jump, write, sing, read

🎨 Adjectives

An adjective is a word that describes a noun.

Examples: beautiful, tall, happy, red, intelligent
